Keynote Topics

Signature Talks

Each keynote is tailored to your audience and can be delivered virtually or in-person. Below are Joan's core signature talks — spanning space exploration, diversity, resilience, and professional growth.

01
Navigating the Universe: A History of Science Exploration Missions
Joan takes audiences on a compelling journey through the evolution of science exploration missions, from the earliest robotic explorers to the sophisticated deep-space observatories redefining our understanding of the universe today. Through powerful stories of ambition, failure, resilience, and discovery, she connects the legacy of space exploration to the next generation of scientists and engineers.
✦ Perfect For
Educational Conferences · Universities & Academic Institutions · Corporate Innovation & Leadership Events · Space & Science Outreach Programs · Women in STEM & Mentorship Events
02
Space for Everyone: The Importance of Diversity in Space
Joan explores why the future of space exploration depends on who gets to participate in it. This talk goes beyond buzzwords, examining the tangible link between diversity, technical excellence, and resilient teams. She addresses systemic barriers while offering practical strategies for building inclusive cultures that attract and retain top talent.
✦ Perfect For
STEM & Aerospace Conferences · Corporate Leadership & DEI Initiatives · Universities & Student Organizations · Industry Teams & Executive Retreats
03
Minorities in STEM: Closing the Skills Gap
As a Latina engineer in aerospace, Joan brings a unique perspective on one of the most pressing challenges in STEM today: the skills gap and the untapped potential within underrepresented communities to close it. She offers actionable strategies for organizations, educators, and leaders to cultivate inclusive talent pipelines.
✦ Perfect For
Corporate & Industry Leaders · STEM Conferences & Workforce Development Summits · Universities & STEM Programs · DEI Initiatives
04
Reaching for the Stars: Achieving Cosmic Success Through Hardships
Joan shares her journey through aerospace and space industries, revealing how resilience, determination, and perseverance can turn challenges into opportunities. Drawing from experiences at NASA and Blue Origin, she inspires audiences to embrace challenges as stepping stones, cultivate grit, and develop the mindset needed to excel in demanding careers.
✦ Perfect For
STEM & Aerospace Conferences · Corporate Leadership & Team Development · Universities & Student Organizations · Industry Teams & Executive Retreats
05
Humanizing the STEM Profession: A Guide Through Failure
Joan shines a light on the human side of STEM, sharing candid stories of challenges, setbacks, and failures from her journey through aerospace and space engineering. She demonstrates that failure isn't a roadblock, it's a critical part of growth. Audiences gain practical strategies to navigate obstacles and foster a culture where learning thrives.
✦ Perfect For
STEM & Aerospace Conferences · Industry Teams & Leadership Programs · Universities & Mentorship Organizations · DEI Initiatives
06
How to Build Your Professional Brand in Aerospace
Joan shares actionable strategies for standing out and thriving in the competitive aerospace industry. She guides audiences through building credibility, networking effectively, and communicating their expertise in ways that open doors to leadership roles and long-term career growth — emphasizing mentorship, advocacy, and representation.
✦ Perfect For
STEM & Aerospace Conferences · Early to Mid-Career Professionals · University & Graduate Students in STEM · Corporate Leadership & Talent Development · Women & Underrepresented Groups in STEM
