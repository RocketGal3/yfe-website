Keynote Topics

Signature Talks

Joan delivers 6* signature keynote talks covering space exploration, diversity in STEM, resilience, and professional growth. Each talk can be tailored to your audience and delivered virtually or in-person.

01
Navigating the Universe: A History of Science Exploration Missions
Joan takes audiences on a compelling journey through the evolution of science exploration missions, from the earliest robotic explorers to the sophisticated deep-space observatories redefining our understanding of the universe today.
✦ Perfect For
Educational Conferences · Universities · Corporate Innovation Events · Space & Science Outreach · Women in STEM Events
02
Space for Everyone: The Importance of Diversity in Space
Joan explores why the future of space exploration depends on who gets to participate in it — examining the tangible link between diversity, technical excellence, and resilient teams.
✦ Perfect For
STEM & Aerospace Conferences · Corporate DEI Initiatives · Universities · Executive Retreats
03
Minorities in STEM: Closing the Skills Gap
As a Latina engineer in aerospace, Joan brings a unique perspective on the skills gap and the untapped potential within underrepresented communities to close it.
✦ Perfect For
Corporate Leaders · STEM Conferences · Universities · DEI Initiatives
04
Reaching for the Stars: Achieving Cosmic Success Through Hardships
Joan shares her journey through aerospace and space industries, revealing how resilience, determination, and perseverance can turn challenges into opportunities.
✦ Perfect For
STEM & Aerospace Conferences · Corporate Leadership · Universities · Executive Retreats
05
Humanizing the STEM Profession: A Guide Through Failure
Joan shines a light on the human side of STEM, sharing candid stories of challenges, setbacks, and failures — demonstrating that failure is a critical part of growth.
✦ Perfect For
STEM & Aerospace Conferences · Leadership Programs · Universities · DEI Initiatives
06
How to Build Your Professional Brand in Aerospace
Joan shares actionable strategies for standing out in the competitive aerospace industry — building credibility, networking effectively, and communicating expertise that opens doors.
✦ Perfect For
STEM & Aerospace Conferences · Early to Mid-Career Professionals · University Students · Women in STEM
* Every keynote topic can be fully tailored to your event, audience, industry, and goals. Joan works closely with organizers to ensure maximum impact.
